TECH VAULT SRI LANKA - OFFICIAL STORE POLICIES AND WARRANTY MANUAL

1. WARRANTY RULES
All laptops above 200,000 LKR include a 3-year comprehensive hardware warranty covering the motherboard, battery, and internal cooling system.
Monitors include a 2-year panel warranty. If a monitor develops 3 or more dead pixels within the first 30 days of purchase, the customer is entitled to an immediate free replacement.
Accessories such as a mouse carry a 6-month replacement warranty against sensor or click-switch failure. Physical damage or liquid spills void all warranties immediately.

2. DELIVERY AND SHIPPING IN SRI LANKA
Standard courier delivery within Colombo takes 1 business day and costs 500 LKR.
Outstation delivery to Anuradhapura, Kandy, and Galle takes 2 to 3 business days and costs 1,200 LKR.
Any single order with a final VAT-inclusive price above 250,000 LKR qualifies for free insured express shipping anywhere in Sri Lanka.

3. RETURNS AND REFUNDS
Unopened items in original sealed packaging may be returned within 7 days for a full refund. Once a laptop or monitor box is unsealed, returns are only accepted for verified factory defects.
